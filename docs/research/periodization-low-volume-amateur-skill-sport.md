# Periodization Signal At 1-3 Sessions/Week For Amateur Skill-Sport

## Agent Quick Scan

- **Bottom line.** Three independent external vendors converge hard on one thing: **classical _physical_ periodization is below the noise floor** for adult recreational, self-coached, skill-sport athletes training 1-3 sessions/week. At this volume the inter-session interval (48-72h) fully restores the athlete, so fatigue/recovery is never the binding constraint and the supercompensation / residual-training-effect machinery of Bompa/Issurin/conjugate models has nothing to manage. No randomized controlled trial tests multi-week periodization against non-periodization for **technical-skill** outcomes in this population — every periodization verdict is extrapolation. The genuine disagreement is narrower: **does the _skill / structural_ mechanism (consolidation cadence, contextual-interference progression) carry enough signal to justify building a multi-week layer, or is the only defensible justification adherence?**
- **Per-vendor headline.** **Vendor 1** (most skeptical): physical *and* skill null; contextual interference is largely a "myth" in applied sport and can reverse at low dose; a layer is defensible **only** on adherence grounds; recommended starting point is the lightest possible (a multi-week "skill focus" label + distributed scheduling + re-tests), graduating only on A/B data. **Vendor 2** (middle): classical macro-structure is below the noise floor, but a **real light micro-structure signal** exists — spacing across days, graded (not maximal) practice variability, representative task design, autoregulation, milestone framing; ship a light rolling 4-week emphasis window **or nothing**, and shipping nothing is "scientifically respectable." **Vendor 3** (most affirmative): a strong, decision-grade **skill-consolidation + progressive-CI** signal exists; periodization is also a potent Self-Determination-Theory adherence mechanism; a multi-week layer **must be built** — a 3-4 week skill-thematic "consolidation cycle."
- **What it changes in this repo.** Nothing today; this is decision-grounding for **`O2`** (how opinionated should early multi-week planning be) and **M002** carry-forward shape. It **reclaims complexity budget**: it kills the "classical periodization is inherently superior" justification for a heavy Phase 1.5 plan-builder. If `O2` activates and any above-session structure ships, the evidence says keep it **light, skill/adherence-framed, all-skills-alive-weekly, and load-agnostic** (leave load to the settled session engine). It **validates** the PoST direction in `docs/research/periodization-post-framework.md` (skill periodization ≠ physical periodization) and the session/multi-week boundary in `docs/research/srpe-load-adaptation-rules.md`.
- **Striking convergence (even the skeptic and the believer agree on the shape).** *If* a layer is built, all three independently land on the same minimum-viable structure: **~3-6 weeks (≈4 weeks / 6-12 exposures)**, sequenced by **technical theme not physiological quality**, intra-block progression from **low → high contextual interference**, **every core skill kept alive each week** (decay-driven maintenance, not concentrate-one-quality-per-block), **autoregulated day-to-day** delivery (the existing session engine), and **simple re-test / carry-forward triggers**. The disagreement is purely about *whether to build it at all and how confidently*, not about its shape.
- **What would flip it.** See [Synthesis stability](#synthesis-stability-what-would-change-this). The decision-grade evidence does not exist in the literature and will not arrive from more literature search (all three say so); the next quality-increasing move is the product's **own cohort A/B** (skill retention + adherence), which would be the most authoritative evidence in existence for this exact population.
- **Scope.** 3-of-3 vendors folded in (all received 2026-06-02). Vendor numbers assigned by stance (skeptic → affirmative) to make the reconciliation spectrum legible; the source-file mapping is recorded in [Scope and provenance](#scope-and-provenance).

## Executive conclusion

Three independent vendor memos, arriving from partly different literatures, converge on a two-part verdict and disagree only on the second part's strength.

**Part 1 — unanimous, high confidence: classical _physical_ periodization is below the noise floor at 1-3 sessions/week.** All three vendors independently conclude that the periodization evidence base is overwhelmingly strength/power, mostly in trained or higher-frequency or coached populations, and that the between-model differences are small even there. The mechanism fails structurally at low volume: with 48-72h between sessions the athlete is fully recovered, so cumulative-fatigue management, supercompensation windows, and residual-training-effect logic have nothing to act on. Volume-equated meta-analyses show the periodization advantage concentrated in *trained* (not untrained/low-volume) participants. And — decisively — **no RCT tests multi-week periodization vs non-periodization for technical-skill outcomes in adult recreational self-coached athletes at this volume.** Every per-shape verdict is extrapolation, and all three vendors say so plainly. The honest reading: any product choice in this layer justified by *physiology* is on speculative ground.

**Part 2 — where they split: does the _skill / structural_ mechanism justify building a multi-week layer?**

- **Vendor 1 (most skeptical):** No. Physical *and* skill effects are both below the noise floor (confidence moderate-to-high on the null). It is uniquely skeptical of contextual interference — reading the applied-sport CI benefit as largely a "myth" that can reverse at low practice dose — and therefore recommends **blocked-leaning** practice when introducing a new skill. A multi-week layer is defensible *only* on adherence/behavioral grounds, and the recommended starting point is the lightest version, graduating to a 4-week skill-focus cycle only if the product's own A/B data show an adherence lift.
- **Vendor 2 (middle):** Classical macro-structure is below the noise floor as a default, **but** a real light *micro-structure* signal exists — distributed practice across days, moderate and progressively increasing variability, representative task design, session-level autoregulation, and simple milestone framing. It treats CI as *conditional* ("graded interference," not maximum randomness) rather than a myth. Ship either no block layer or a light rolling 4-week emphasis window; it states explicitly that **shipping without a multi-week planner is a scientifically respectable decision, not a cop-out.**
- **Vendor 3 (most affirmative):** A strong, decision-grade skill signal exists. It argues motor-skill *systems consolidation* (days-to-weeks, sleep-dependent), the **Challenge Point Framework**, **progressive contextual interference** (blocked → serial → random across weeks), and the **constraint-led approach** carry measurable skill-retention/transfer effects that *must* be progressed over multiple weeks — and that periodization is independently a potent Self-Determination-Theory adherence mechanism. It concludes a multi-week layer **must be built**.

**Part 3 — the convergence that matters for product.** Despite disagreeing on whether to build, all three describe the *same* minimum-viable structure if one is built (see [Reconciliation](#reconciliation)): a ~4-week skill-**thematic** block (not a physiological accumulation/intensification/realization arc), with intra-block progression from low to high contextual interference, **all core skills kept alive every week** (because skill decays without touch — explicitly anti-block-concentration), day-to-day intensity left to the autoregulated session engine, and simple end-of-cycle re-tests. And all three agree the **single most robust lever is spacing** (distributed > massed practice across days), and that **adherence** — goal-gradient, milestone anticipation, plan continuity, SDT competence — is the best-evidenced (if indirect) justification for any above-session structure.

**Practical implication for this repo.** The evidence does *not* support building a heavy, physiologically-justified multi-week planner for M002 / Phase 1.5. It *does* support — if anything is built — a light, skill-organization-and-adherence-framed layer that matches `O2`'s existing "weekly confidence layer, not a scheduling canvas" lean. The "below noise floor for physical periodization" finding is itself decision-grade: it reclaims the complexity budget a mesocycle engine would have consumed and tells the team to default to the cheaper, shallower structure (`D15` / `D22`'s "shallow next-N queue, not a periodized season builder") and to validate any richer layer with its own A/B data rather than borrowed elite sports science.

## Per-vendor evidence ladders

### Vendor 1 evidence ladder (2026-06-02) — most skeptical

Condensed from [`docs/research/briefs/responses/2026-06-02-periodization-low-volume-amateur-skill-sport-vendor-1.md`](./briefs/responses/2026-06-02-periodization-low-volume-amateur-skill-sport-vendor-1.md). Full citations and caveats live in that file.

| Evidence layer | Bearing on "build a multi-week layer?" | Representative anchor (N / effect) | Why it applies (or doesn't) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Target-population skill RCT | — (absent) | None located; a targeted search for block/linear/DUP trials with technical-skill outcomes in adult amateurs returned **none** | The study that would settle the question does not exist. Physical null is therefore an extrapolation, explicitly flagged. |
| Resistance-training periodization meta-analysis | Weak; physical effect small and trained-only | Williams et al. 2017 (*Sports Med* 47:2083-2100): periodized > non-periodized 1RM, **18 studies / 81 effects**, d≈0.43, attenuating to **d≈0.23** after correction; advantage concentrated in trained/higher-frequency | Not skill, not low-volume amateur. "Peaking" assumes a target event recreational players lack. |
| Linear vs undulating equivalence | Undercuts "model choice matters" | Harries et al. 2015: **no significant LP-vs-UP difference**; Rhea et al. 2002 (**N=20 men, 12 wks, 3×/wk**): DUP bench +28.78% vs LP +14.37% — but strength only, DUP edge null in *untrained* (Moesgaard 2022) | At this volume "block" collapses into progressive overload. |
| Contextual interference (the skeptical read) | **Against** aggressive randomization | Brady 2004 (**61 studies / 139 ES**): overall d=0.38 but **applied d=0.19 vs lab d=0.57**, adults d=0.50 vs young d=0.10; Ammar 2024 (**933 records → 36 studies, 183 outcomes**): only **37/183 (20%)** agreed with the CI prediction, pre-post ES=0.1 (p=0.35); Ammar 2023 "myth"; **Verwey 2022: random benefit reverses (blocked > random) under extended practice** | The most decision-relevant motor-learning finding for vendor 1: heavy interleaving may *impair* early acquisition of a genuinely new skill; novices often do better with blocked practice. |
| Spacing / distributed practice | **For** (the one robust lever) | Shea et al. 2000; Dail & Christina 2004 (**RQES 75:148-155**, novice golf putting: distributed > massed at acquisition *and* retention to 28 days) | A multi-week layer's most defensible job is simply preserving repeated exposures across days. A *spacing* finding, not a *block* finding. |
| Skill-consolidation timeline | Anchors "months, not a constant" | Williams & Hodges 2023: explicit lack of evidence on sport-skill long-term retention; consolidation research confined to lab tasks. Real interventions run **6-12 weeks at 2-3×/wk** | Corroborates the "~3 months" volleyball anchor as an order-of-magnitude heuristic, not a validated constant. |
| Adherence mechanism (indirect) | **For** (only positively-evidenced justification) | Kivetz/Urminsky/Zheng 2006 goal-gradient; Hull 1932; BCT adherence meta SMD≈0.24 (I²=0%); "flexible beats rigid" app-design framing | The *only* mechanism with positive (if indirect) evidence for the target population. Conditional on the structure being flexible and forgiving (friction risk is real). |
| Practitioner intuition | Mixed; converges on "keep all skills alive" | Volleyball coaches: *"I would not want my players going 3-4 days without serving and passing"* (explicitly anti-block); climbing: LP for beginners, UP/block reserved for advanced; tennis/racquet S&C: DUP for the *physical* side, not stroke skill | Where coaches keep skills alive weekly and individualize by feel they converge with the evidence; where they sell 12-week peaking macrocycles to weekend players they diverge. |

**Vendor 1 verdict table (condensed):** Linear *does not translate*; Block *does not translate* (collapses to progressive overload below the elite-volume floor); DUP *partially translates as intra-week variety only*; Conjugate *does not translate / insufficient*; Contextual interference *partially translates, can reverse at low dose*; Constraint-led *partially translates as session-design philosophy, not a multi-week layer*; Autoregulated ***translates*** (and is already built); No periodization / progressive overload ***translates — the default winner.*** Recommended minimum-viable structure if built: 4-week skill-focus cycles (range 3-6), theme rotation with all skills maintained, blocked-leaning for new skills shifting to interleaved, enforced spacing, simple end-of-cycle re-test — but the **recommended starting point is the lighter "skill focus label + spacing + re-tests" with no formal block model.**

### What the reconciliation does not change

- **No decision in `docs/decisions.md` is modified by this note.** It grounds `O2` (still a Phase-1.5 open question), informs M002 shape, and extends what a `D91`-style cohort should instrument. The vendor evidence does not authorize unilateral changes to any `D*`.
- **No code changes land from this distillation.** The settled session-level engine (`srpe-load-adaptation-rules.md`) is untouched and explicitly endorsed as the right home for load.
- **The synthesis does not manufacture a "build it" consensus.** Two of three vendors are skeptical-to-conditional and the unanimous finding is the physical null; the reconciled posture is *do not build heavy; if you build, build light and minimal and validate with your own data.*

## Synthesis stability: what would change this

Pre-registered so future evidence (a vendor 4, a new study, or internal cohort data) can be evaluated against an explicit bar rather than re-litigating the whole synthesis.

- **Bar 1 — would justify a physiologically-framed periodization engine.** A target-population RCT (adult, 2-5 yrs, self-coached, skill-sport, 1-3×/wk) showing a measurable **physical/load-periodization** advantage over simple progressive overload. *Status: nothing approaches it; all three vendors say it will not come from more literature search.* If it lands, reopen the physical-mechanism justification — but weigh it against the structural argument that recovery is not the constraint at this volume.
- **Bar 2 — would upgrade the skill case from "plausible" to "build."** A low-volume, **long-horizon (6-12 month)** RCT showing progressive-CI / skill-thematic blocking beats simple session sequencing on *retained* skill (not 48h acquisition) in adult rec amateurs. *Status: only short-horizon (3-day) novice data exists (vendor 3's N=84), one step removed.* If it lands, move the reconciled "skill signal: plausible" to "established."
- **Bar 3 — the decision-grade internal lever (adherence).** A product A/B showing a light multi-week layer lifts second-session start / self-initiated return vs a session-only loop would justify investing further; a null result or increased drop-off (the friction risk all three flag) would justify *removing* it. This is the most authoritative evidence obtainable for this exact population and should be the actual gate, per all three vendors.
- **Bar 4 — CI sign at low volume.** Internal data showing that imposing variability early *hurts* new-skill acquisition would harden the "blocked-early, progress-to-variable" rule (vendor 1's caution); data showing early interleaving helps would soften it toward vendor 3.

### Where the current synthesis is fragile (named, not hidden)

1. **No vendor has target-population evidence.** The entire skill/physical verdict is extrapolation from novice lab tasks, strength training, youth/coached team sport, and behavior-change literature. A single well-designed direct study would outweigh the current stack.
2. **Vendor 3's strongest affirmative anchors are short-horizon and partly image-stripped.** The N=84 progressive-CI result is a 3-day novice study; the recreational-athlete adherence trial's exact N and motivation scores were rendered as images and stripped on filing. Treat vendor 3's specific quantitative claims as directional; recover exact figures from the original deliverable if they ever gate a decision.
3. **The CI literature is in open methodological dispute** (Ammar "myth" papers vs Czyż retention-benefit papers). The reconciled "conditional/graded" reading is the safe synthesis but is not a settled scientific consensus.
4. **Adherence evidence is all indirect.** It comes from behavior-change, mHealth, and UX literature, not skill-sport periodization RCTs, and there is *no* peer-reviewed evidence isolating a multi-week *planner feature's* causal impact in skill sport.

## Decision implications

### `O2` — how opinionated should early multi-week planning be?

This is the decision the brief most directly serves. The evidence **sharpens `O2`'s existing directional lean rather than overturning it.** `O2` already leans toward "a weekly confidence layer and a main-tool habit, not a scheduling canvas," with iteration through structured feedback and AI as question-asker/translator. The vendor synthesis adds three constraints:

1. **Justify the layer on skill-organization + adherence, never on physiology.** A physiologically-framed mesocycle engine is below the noise floor at this volume. This *narrows* how opinionated the layer may be: it may organize skill themes and surface a progression arc, but it must not claim to optimize physical supercompensation.
2. **Keep it light and keep all skills alive.** The reconciled minimum-viable shape (≈4-week skill-thematic block, low→high CI, all skills maintained weekly, autoregulated delivery, simple re-test) is the ceiling of defensible opinionatedness, not a floor to build toward. Two of three vendors recommend starting *below* even that — a "current focus + spacing + re-test" surface with no formal block model.
3. **Gate richer structure on the product's own A/B (Bar 3), not on borrowed sports science.** The most authoritative evidence for this population is the product's own cohort.

### M002 (Weekly Confidence Loop) carry-forward shape

The brief flags this synthesis as gating the Phase-1.5 plan-builder go/no-go and compounding into M002 carry-forward continuity framing. Bottom line for M002: **do not make a heavy multi-week planner M002 core.** If M002 surfaces any above-session structure (next-N queue, weekly receipt, carry-forward arc per `D124`), keep it (a) shallow — consistent with `D15`/`D22`'s "next 2-6 sessions queue, not a periodized season builder"; (b) framed by weekly confidence + adherence + skill-theme continuity, not physiology; (c) all-skills-alive weekly; (d) load-agnostic, deferring to the session engine. The vendors independently propose the same ~12-week, 3-arm RCT (progressive overload vs light skill-block vs free/autoregulated), measuring **both** skill retention/transfer **and** adherence (session completion, self-initiated return, SDT competence). This should inform what a post-`D91` / `D130`-window cohort captures *if a multi-week layer is ever tested*: instrument skill retention/transfer **and** adherence dimensions, and classify returns as self-initiated vs prompted — extending `D91`'s existing instrumentation requirement rather than replacing it. The product's own cohort would be the most authoritative target-population evidence in existence.

## Gaps the literature does not close

All three vendors converge on the same gaps — and the convergence is itself decision-grade (it means more literature search will not resolve the core question):

1. **No target-population RCT.** No trial compares periodized vs non-periodized **skill** training in adult recreational self-coached athletes at 1-3 sessions/week with retention/transfer *and* adherence outcomes over 8-12+ weeks. This is the single study that would close the gap; it does not exist.
2. **No validated skill-consolidation constant.** The "~3 months at 2-3×/wk" anchor is a practitioner heuristic corroborated in magnitude but not as a measured law; consolidation research is confined to lab tasks (manual aiming, sequencing).
3. **The contextual-interference effect at low volume is genuinely unresolved** — an open methodological dispute (Ammar "myth" vs Czyż retention benefit), with dose-dependent sign reversal evidence (Verwey 2022) complicating any clean rule.
4. **Digital multi-week planner causal impact is a black box.** No peer-reviewed study isolates the effect of a multi-week planner vs a session-by-session generator in open-skill sport; the supporting evidence is associative behavior-change/UX literature and proprietary app telemetry.
5. **Adherence-mechanism vs skill-mechanism cannot be separated by existing evidence.** Distinguishing "the layer helps because it organizes skill" from "the layer helps because it sustains adherence" requires a factorial design nobody has run — and the two justify *different* product designs (a skill-organization layer vs a motivational-scaffold layer).

The honest reading: the literature is good enough to **reject a heavy, physiologically-justified multi-week planner as the default**, and good enough to **endorse spacing + a light, flexible, skill/adherence-framed structure if one is built** — but not good enough to claim any specific block shape is *superior* in this exact population. The decisive evidence is the product's own A/B, not the next literature search.
