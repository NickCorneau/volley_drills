# M002 Evidence Meta-Synthesis (2026-06-02 four-topic research day)

## Agent Quick Scan

- **What this is.** A cross-cutting readout of four independent vendor-research syntheses filed on 2026-06-02 — [single-tracked-metric](./single-tracked-metric-amateur-skill-apps.md), [subjective-skill-confidence-validity](./subjective-skill-confidence-validity.md), [periodization-low-volume](./periodization-low-volume-amateur-skill-sport.md), and [coach-pedagogy-translation](./coach-pedagogy-translation-self-coached.md) — read **together** for what they jointly say about M002. Each owning synthesis remains authoritative for its own topic; this note is the join, not a replacement.
- **The one combined posture all four converge on.** M002 should be a **calm, two-signal weekly loop** where: (1) the **honest headline progress signal is behavioral** — planned-vs-completed adherence — not a confidence number; (2) **weekly confidence is a relabeled felt-readiness / engagement companion**, never surfaced as "your skill improved"; (3) any **above-session structure stays a light skill-thematic queue** (spacing + keep-all-skills-alive), never a periodized/physiological planner; (4) any **goal / promise / cue surfaces are user-owned, process-framed, off the active RunScreen, and A/B-gated**, never coach-graded pass/fail or objective capability guarantees.
- **The cross-cutting tension (the headline finding).** The milestone is named **"Weekly Confidence Loop"** and centers confidence as the skill signal — and **all four sets independently undercut that exact center of gravity.** The metric note and confidence-validity note say confidence is a contested, directionally-biased skill proxy; the pedagogy note makes the forward-looking confidence *promise* the weakest, A/B-gate-only piece; the periodization note says the multi-week ambition should stay deliberately thin. The evidence validates the *loop* and challenges the *confidence-as-skill-signal* framing.
- **Validates** (build with confidence): the pure **thin-spine plan** (ideation survivor #2), the **deferral of anchor / event / taper** (survivor #1 follow-on), the **two-metric weekly receipt**, **markdown-first / no-AI-plan-gen**, and the **calm-not-calendar** posture.
- **Challenges** (re-scope or re-frame before building): the **"Weekly Confidence Loop" name + confidence-as-skill-metric**; the **cue-ladder's mastery-gating** (survivor #3 — "advance on demonstrated execution" reads as coach-graded pass/fail, which backfires coachlessly); any **un-gated outcome promise** (`O23`).
- **Founder advancement-signal question, answered.** The founder's #1 ask — *"see adaptations over time … know I'm at least 1% better"* — cannot be honestly carried by confidence alone (it is biased and can move opposite to skill at the 2-5-yr band). The believable "1% better" signal everyone points to is a **repeated structured drill score on a focal skill** (at a courtside-capture cost). The honest v1 carries felt-progress with a **behavioral receipt + the thin-spine's visible carry-forward / offered-adaptation loop** (survivor #5) and a **clean seam** for the drill score, with confidence as the readiness companion.
- **Canon handling.** Recommend-only. No `D*`/`O*` is modified, no schema/scope change is authorized. The rename, the `O22`/`O23` resolutions, and the v1 advancement-signal choice are **founder-ratify** items, listed below and in the companion red-team memo.
- **Scope.** Cross-synthesis of four 3-of-N notes (all received 2026-06-02). The red-team / revalidation of the M002 ideation against this evidence lives in [`docs/reviews/2026-06-02-m002-ideation-revalidation.md`](../reviews/2026-06-02-m002-ideation-revalidation.md).

|---|---|---|

## What the four sets jointly say (the convergences)

These are claims **no single note owns alone** — they emerge from reading the four together.

1. **Behavioral-primary, subjective-companion.** Three of the four sets independently push the same hierarchy: the trustworthy progress signal at this dose is **behavioral** (planned-vs-completed adherence; deliberate-practice volume; a repeated drill score), and **subjective self-rating is a readiness/engagement companion, never the skill headline**. The metric note ranks planned-vs-completed first; the confidence-validity note forbids the objective-skill claim for confidence; the pedagogy note shows the *promise* about confidence is the weakest, most-gated piece. This is the single strongest cross-cutting result.
2. **Keep the multi-week layer thin, and justify it on adherence + skill-organization, never physiology.** The periodization note kills the physiological case for a heavy planner; the metric and pedagogy notes both warn that added structure/ceremony is itself an abandonment driver. Together they say: the thin-spine is not a compromise, it is the **evidence-backed ceiling** for v1 — richer structure must earn its place on the product's own A/B.
3. **Spacing + continuity is the defensible "plan" mechanism.** The one robust periodization lever (distributed practice across days, all skills kept alive) is exactly what a thin-spine "intentions + cadence + ready backlog ordered by staleness" already delivers. The plan's honest job is **preserving exposures and surfacing what's gone stale**, not optimizing a mesocycle.
4. **Ownership is load-bearing; AI-authored content collapses it.** The pedagogy note's Schimpf N=470 result (app-authored goals score higher on quality but collapse adherence 72.8% → 46.6%) and the metric/periodization "co-authored beats auto-generated" framing jointly reinforce `P7` (no AI-generated plans) and the markdown-first / user-owned posture of the ideation. The authoring friction is the feature.
5. **Instrument from day one — the product's own cohort is the missing study.** All four notes flag the target population as under-researched and converge on the same move: log the signals now (planned-vs-completed, a skill proxy, confidence + mood/fatigue/last-outcome covariates) so the `D91` cohort and the 2026-07-20 `D130` re-eval read real dose-response, not extrapolation. Every quantitative claim across all four is explicitly extrapolated.
6. **Calm beats dense.** Noise-floor discipline (rolling windows, no precise weekly deltas), no streaks-as-hero, no calendar-guilt, off-RunScreen placement of any new framing copy — the four notes independently land on the founder's existing calm/shibui posture as the *evidence-correct* design, not just a taste preference.

## What the evidence VALIDATES in the current M002 ideation

Mapped to the 2026-06-02 plan-and-adaptation ideation survivors and the M002 milestone.

- **Survivor #2 — plan-as-thin-spine (the selected seed), pure-spine scope.** Directly validated. Periodization says don't build heavy; metric + confidence say keep the receipt calm and behavioral-anchored; spacing/continuity is exactly the thin-spine's job. The "only the next session is concrete, backlog ordered by staleness/autoregulation" shape is the evidence-backed ceiling, not a thin compromise.
- **Deferring the anchor / event / taper (survivor #1 as follow-on).** Validated. The periodization physical-null specifically reclaims the taper/peaking budget; "calm not calendar" + no countdown-guilt matches the metric note's anti-streak / anti-calendar-guilt findings.
- **Two-metric weekly receipt (planned-vs-completed + one skill proxy).** Validated by the metric note as the correct shape (a pair, kept distinct, never summed), with planned-vs-completed as the anchor.
- **Markdown-first emission / no AI plan generation.** Validated by the pedagogy ownership evidence and the cross-cutting "co-authored beats auto-generated."
- **Survivor #7 — structured warmup content block.** Validated as a low-cost, in-vision win; spacing/exposure framing supports a high-frequency warmup, and it fills an already-authorized-but-thin `warmup` focus.
- **Survivor #5 — offered (pull-not-push) adaptation loop with a verdict ledger.** Validated and *elevated* by this evidence: it is the honest answer to the founder's "see it adapting" ask (see below), it respects ownership (`P7`), and "offered, not imposed" matches the adherence/forgiveness findings across the metric and periodization notes.

## What the evidence CHALLENGES (re-frame or re-scope before building)

1. **The "Weekly Confidence Loop" name + confidence-as-skill-metric.** Two notes say confidence is a contested, directionally-biased skill proxy; a third makes the confidence *promise* the weakest piece. **Recommendation (founder-ratify):** keep confidence as the **felt-readiness / engagement** half of the receipt, relabel what it is allowed to claim (readiness, never skill proof), make **planned-vs-completed the behavioral headline**, and leave a clean seam for a repeated drill score. Whether to rename the milestone is the founder's call; the *meaning* should be reframed even if the name persists.
2. **Survivor #3 — cue-ladder "mastery-gated progression" (advance on demonstrated execution).** The pedagogy note shows coach-graded pass/fail correction backfires coachlessly (ironic-monitoring / self-criticism). **Recommendation:** build the cue-ladder as a **user-owned, process-framed, exploratory** surface — "try this / see how it feels" + a session-end reflection — not a pass/fail mastery gate. The external-focus cue *content* is validated; the *gating* is what changes.
3. **`O23` — forward-looking outcome promise.** Production effect may be ~0 and an objective promise risks durable trust damage. **Recommendation:** if shipped at all, **A/B-gate it**, frame it as felt readiness ("you should feel more ready / more confident in [skill]") never skill proof, never promise a week-over-week confidence rise, and back it with a behavioral receipt so a flat/declining confidence week is reframed, not read as failure.
4. **`O22` — per-session goal.** Ships, but only as a **user-authored process/open goal anchored to the focus chip with a session-end closure** — never a blank field, never SMART/outcome, never app-generated. The closure loop is load-bearing, and the surface belongs on pre-run / review, not the active RunScreen (≤45-word READ-DO tension).

## The founder advancement-signal question (the #1 ask), answered with the evidence

The founder named the most important thing M002 must do: *"I want to believe / see adaptations to the plan over time … knowing repetition is key but also not wanting to redo the same thing forever … know I'm getting at least 1% better."* The four sets grade the candidate signals:

| Candidate "am I getting better?" signal | Evidence grade | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Self-call / weekly confidence alone** | Weakest objective signal | State self-efficacy, not skill; directionally biased at the 2-5-yr band; can move *opposite* to skill during plateaus (confidence-validity + metric notes). Honest as *readiness/engagement*, dishonest as *proof of improvement*. |
| **Reps / time / sessions done** | Proves activity, not improvement | Adherence ≠ skill; planned-vs-completed is the best *adherence* anchor but is explicitly an *indirect* improvement signal (metric note). |
| **Repeated structured drill score on a focal skill** | The most believable "1% better" | Same task, same scoring rule, measured often enough to beat noise — the objective upgrade all three metric vendors point to and the confidence-validity note names as the honest seam. Cost: real courtside capture friction. |
| **Visible plan adaptation (carry-forward + offered verdict loop)** | The honest v1 "you can see it adapting" surface | Survivor #5 + the thin-spine's visible carry-forward turn scattered captures into a legible, owned training memory; matches "offered, not imposed" adherence evidence and `P7`. Shows *the plan responding to you*, which is what the founder asked to *see*. |

**Reconciled answer.** The felt-progress the founder wants should be carried in v1 by **(a) the thin-spine's visible carry-forward + the offered-adaptation verdict loop (survivor #5)** as the "you can see it adapting, you're not redoing the same thing forever" surface, **(b) a behavioral receipt** (planned-vs-completed) as the consistency story, and **(c) confidence relabeled as a readiness companion** — with a **clean, pre-planned seam for a repeated structured drill score** as the objective "1% better" upgrade once the capture cost is judged worth it. Confidence alone must not be asked to carry "I'm getting better," because the evidence says it cannot do so honestly for this population.
