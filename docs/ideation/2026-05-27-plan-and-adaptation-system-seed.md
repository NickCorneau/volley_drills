# Ideation Seed: Plan-and-Adaptation System

## Why this seed exists

Surfaced 2026-05-27 during the `ce-brainstorm` run on M002 directions ideation survivor #1 (CC-1: Release-notes weekly receipt as dual-read cohort-evidence artifact). When asked what would make the weekly receipt feel like a "training home" on Sunday evening, the founder named a substantially larger vision than CC-1's receipt scope:

> "we have tournaments we want to play towards; or skills we want to improve over a longer time horizon; with timing available and a current active season and pre commitments, and help with scheduling etc and building plans and adapting them over time based on feedback is whats in my mind. but this does not need to be done in this phase either if its too large"

This is materially the Phase 1.5 / coach-clipboard territory M002 explicitly excludes. The founder explicitly permitted parking it. The CC-1 brainstorm continued narrowly; this seed captures the bigger vision so it can drive its own ideation/brainstorm later.

**Authority caveat:** this is a **seed**, not an ideation pass. It captures the founder-verbatim direction and a few obvious shape questions, but did not dispatch the 6 ideation frames. The next move for this topic is `/ce-ideate` (or `/ce-brainstorm` if scope settles fast).

## Founder-verbatim posture (2026-05-27)

These constraints bind any future work in this area:

- **Respect the player's mental attention.** No notifications. No gamification (yet — the "yet" is the founder's qualifier, worth preserving).
- **Every piece of information shown must help the user improve, plan, or prepare.** Stats for the sake of stats are explicitly out.
- **Do not overload.** The founder does not want users seeing things that don't help them, or feeling overwhelmed by information density.
- **Very intentional, calm, respectful, thoughtful.** This is a posture binding on every surface in this area.
- **"This is what I've done" is fine** — retrospective is allowed; what's not allowed is retrospective-without-purpose.

## Named pillars of the vision

The founder named these as the components in their head:

1. **Tournaments to play toward** — discrete future events with dates that anchor planning
2. **Skills to improve over a longer time horizon** — multi-week-to-multi-month skill development arcs
3. **Timing availability** — when the user is actually free to train
4. **Current active season** — a notion of "what season are we in right now" (presumably beach season, indoor season, or whatever the player's competitive context is)
5. **Pre-commitments** — real-life calendar items that constrain training
6. **Help with scheduling** — surface that helps the user place training sessions against pillars 3 + 5
7. **Help with building plans** — taking pillars 1 + 2 and producing an actionable training plan
8. **Help with adapting plans over time based on feedback** — the adaptation loop closes the system; the plan isn't static once built

## Relationship to existing roadmap and M002

- This vision overlaps significantly with what `docs/roadmap.md` calls Phase 1.5 (deeper adaptation, periodization, multi-week planning surfaces) and addresses `O2` (how opinionated multi-week planning should be).
- M002 (Weekly Confidence Loop) is the layer *before* this in the current sequencing. The bigger vision is naturally a Phase 1.5 or M003-tier artifact.
- M002 may end up shipping a small forward-compatible surface (e.g., the CC-1 receipt) that *hints* at this plan context without trying to be it — that's the narrow path the parallel CC-1 brainstorm is now exploring.
- The vision is NOT the coach clipboard (M003 / D72). The coach clipboard is a coach-shares-with-athlete surface; this is a self-coached-or-pair planning system. The two might compound (the coach clipboard later reads the plan), but they are different artifacts.

## Open questions worth thinking through next

These are the obvious shape questions an `/ce-ideate` or `/ce-brainstorm` run on this topic should engage with:

- **Is the season-plan system founder-only at first, or pair-shared from day one?** (D146 pair-first ratified — likely pair-shared, but worth a probe.)
- **Should the plan be authored by the user, generated by the app, or co-authored?** (Implementation-intentions Cohen's d ≈ 0.65 and self-selected habits +37% argue for user-authored or co-authored; auto-generation is the obvious move and may be wrong.)
- **How does the adaptation loop close?** What feedback signal triggers plan changes — perceived improvement, missed sessions, schedule shifts, skill assessments?
- **What's the relationship to existing M001-shipped capture?** (ExecutionLog, SessionReview, sessionSummary — does the plan-system read those, or does it require a parallel capture stream?)
- **What's the smallest viable version?** Is it "one tournament date + one skill goal + one pre-commit calendar" or even smaller?
- **How does it not become a calendar planner?** M002's milestone doc explicitly excludes "full periodized calendar planning" — there's a real line between "useful plan surface" and "calendar planner" that this vision has to land on the right side of.
- **What's the off-ramp?** When the founder doesn't have a tournament or active season, what does the plan-system show / do?

## What this seed is NOT

- It is not an authoritative scope claim for M002 or M003. M002 currently still excludes it per the milestone doc.
- It is not a decision about whether to ship this. The founder explicitly named it as possibly too large for "this phase" (the M002 phase).
- It is not a structural commitment to season/tournament framing as the only valid framing. The eight pillars above are the founder's first articulation, not a final scope.

## Recommended next move

When this seed is picked up, the first move is `/ce-ideate` against the eight pillars (and the founder-posture constraints) to generate direction candidates and revalidations. Do not jump straight to `/ce-brainstorm` — the scope is large enough that direction-level ideation is needed before any one shape gets defined precisely.
