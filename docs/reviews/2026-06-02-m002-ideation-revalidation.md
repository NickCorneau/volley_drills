# M002 Ideation Revalidation Against The 2026-06-02 Research Day

## Why this doc exists

On 2026-06-02 the founder attended a 3-day Better at Beach camp, surfaced field evidence (technique-"how" depth, attack, timing/spacing, 3v3, warmups), and ran a fresh `ce-ideate` pass that produced `docs/ideation/2026-06-02-plan-and-adaptation-system-ideation.md`. The founder selected **survivor #2 (plan-as-thin-spine), pure-spine scope** as the brainstorm seed. The same day, four external research topics returned and were filed as syntheses, then joined in `docs/research/2026-06-02-m002-evidence-meta-synthesis.md`.

This memo is the **red-team + revalidation** the founder asked for: does the selected ideation and the M002 direction still hold once the four-set evidence is in front of us? Five persona reviewers were dispatched via `ce-doc-review` (adversarial, product-lens, scope-guardian, feasibility, coherence — security-lens excluded per the usual local-first/no-auth criteria, matching the 2026-05-27 review). This doc consolidates their findings, gives the verdict, answers the founder's advancement-signal question with the graded evidence, records the safe cross-reference fixes already applied, and lists what only the founder can decide.

It picks up where `docs/reviews/2026-05-27-m002-receipt-doc-review-synthesis.md` left off: that review surfaced a strategic-shape fork (current-shape / max-cut / plan-system slice) as *Resolve Before Planning*. The 2026-06-02 ideation **resolved that fork** by selecting the plan-system slice (thin-spine). This memo revalidates *that resolution* against evidence that landed after it.

**Authority caveat.** Reviewers ran read-only against the live repo. Findings are recommend-only; this memo modifies no `D*`/`O*`, no milestone scope, and no schema. The cross-reference edits applied (see §"Applied fixes") are discoverability-only and change no scope.

## Three convergent strategic concerns

### S1 — The pure-spine (#2 only) defers survivor #5, which the evidence names as the honest v1 carrier of the founder's #1 need (CRITICAL)

**Raised by:** adversarial A1 (CRITICAL) + product-lens P1 (CRITICAL) + scope-guardian SG2.

The founder's loudest cross-session ask (`founder-use-ledger.md` §2026-06-02; ideation §Decision) is to *"believe / see adaptations to the plan over time … know I'm getting at least 1% better."* The meta-synthesis answers that need explicitly with **survivor #5** — the offered-adaptation verdict loop ("accept / keep original") + the thin-spine's visible carry-forward — and *elevates* it: *"the honest answer to the founder's 'see it adapting' ask … shows the plan responding to you, which is what the founder asked to see."* But the ideation's Decision selected **pure thin-spine (#2 only)** and the Handoff relegates #5 to "forward-compatible follow-ons, not v1." The ideation itself concedes #2-without-the-loop *"risks reading as 'M001 + a cadence'"* — a backlog "ordered by staleness/autoregulation" is a queue, not an adaptation the user can *see responding to them*. The milestone's own goal #4 ("feel that something is in the book") and its in-scope "visible carry-forward" + "bounded deterministic explanation for why the next session stayed the same, got lighter, or got harder" both center visible adaptation — the brainstorm seed is the doc that cut it.

**Recommendation (founder-ratify):** pull the **minimal** offered-verdict slice of #5 — one "accept / keep original" delta + the visible carry-forward — into M002 v1 alongside the spine, and keep the heavier append-only verdict-ledger / threshold machinery deferred. If #5 stays fully deferred, the brainstorm should name which concrete v1 surface carries the "1% better / it's adapting" need, or state plainly that v1 is a deliberate substrate-first step that does not yet serve the #1 need. Do not let "forward-compatible" silently stand in for "served."

### S2 — Confidence-as-skill-signal is encoded in the name, the planning defaults, and the receipt slots, and all four sets contradict it (CRITICAL)

**Raised by:** adversarial A2 (CRITICAL) + product-lens P2 (HIGH) + scope-guardian SG4 + coherence C1 (CRITICAL) + C2.

All four research sets independently undercut confidence as a skill proxy: the confidence-validity note (it measures felt readiness, *"should not be surfaced as 'your skill is improving'"*, can move **opposite** to skill in the 2-5-yr band), the single-tracked-metric note (confidence *"Low and possibly NEGATIVE for improvement"*), and the pedagogy note (the forward-looking confidence *promise* is the weakest, A/B-gate-only piece). The risk is not only the milestone **name**: the milestone's **planning defaults are written as implementer instructions** that now contradict the evidence — `m002-weekly-confidence-loop.md` line 114 (*"Weekly confidence matters more than rich history"*) and line 116 (*"a weekly receipt is a confidence and investment surface"*). Coherence C2 adds a second slot conflict: the milestone's receipt lists *"one load proxy"* (lines 73, 83), but the metric note is unanimous that user-facing sRPE/load is below the noise floor — *"keep sRPE as backend telemetry only"* — and the meta-synthesis describes a **two-metric** receipt (planned-vs-completed + one skill proxy) with no user-facing load proxy. This is the same inherited-convention failure mode the 2026-05-27 review caught for cadence (S2 there): a framing survived because the milestone name said so, not because evidence chose it.

**Recommendation (founder-ratify):** reframe what confidence is *allowed to claim* — a felt-readiness / engagement companion, never skill proof — and make **planned-vs-completed the behavioral headline**, with a clean seam for a repeated drill score. Renaming "Weekly Confidence Loop" is the founder's call; the *meaning* should be corrected regardless of the name. Reconcile the receipt to two user-facing metrics (drop or backend-only the load proxy). These are the meta-synthesis's #1 founder-ratify items.

### S3 — Deferring the entire content/technique cluster runs against the primary new signal (HIGH)

**Raised by:** product-lens P3 (HIGH) + P4 (HIGH) + P5 (MED-HIGH); scope-guardian SG7 concurs on holding the *collision-prone* parts but agrees #7 is a blessed low-cost exception.

The ideation designates the BAB camp as *"the primary new signal,"* and that signal is overwhelmingly **content**: technique-"how" depth (*"I never really knew how to set until now"* — *"the loudest camp signal"*), attack, timing/spacing, 3v3, warmups. The critical distinction the reviewers surface: the periodization "keep it thin" finding applies to the **multi-week planner**, *not* to **content** — no synthesis says defer content; the evidence shouts the opposite. Deferring taper/event is evidence-backed; deferring *all* content is a scope choice that runs against the primary signal. And the founder explicitly licensed bolder, in-vision scope (*"okay to not take it too safe as long as it's fully in line with our vision"*) — yet the lowest-ambition option was chosen, while the two highest-confidence in-vision content survivors carry **no canon cost**: cue-ladder (#3, 88% confidence) is depth on existing focuses, and structured warmup (#7) fills the already-authorized-but-empty `warmup` focus. Only roster-count (#4) reopens `D101`/`D143`/`D144`. Product-lens P5 adds the trajectory stake: `STRATEGY.md` ties founder-willingness-to-recommend (the qualitative threshold that unlocks the cohort question at the 2026-07-20 `D130` re-eval) to *"gaps in content, planning, and progression-over-time"* — a content-free M002 that reads as "M001 + a cadence" may not move that threshold by the date it sits on.

**Recommendation (founder-ratify):** pair the thin-spine with at least the cheapest content win — **survivor #7 (warmup)** as a near-free, in-vision, already-authorized addition — and seriously weigh **survivor #3 (cue-ladder) in the evidence-approved *exploratory* framing** ("try this / see how it feels" + session-end reflection, **never** the mastery-gated pass/fail the pedagogy note rejects) as the headline content bet that closes the #1 field gap. Keep the canon-colliding content (attack/3v3 = `D143`/`D101`/`D144`) correctly deferred to its decision-packet path.

## Load-bearing feasibility findings (codebase-grounded)

These refine the meta-synthesis with what the actual code supports. They do not change the verdict; they constrain *how* the recommendations land.

- **F1 — The repeated drill score is seam-only, and the binding constraint is NOT RunScreen copy.** Capture happens on `/run/check` (`DrillCheckScreen`), not the active RunScreen, so the ≤45-word READ-DO/DO-CONFIRM constraint is not the blocker the meta-synthesis implies. The real blockers are (a) the Phase 2B capture shapes (`points-to-target` / `pass-grade-avg`) are gated behind founder-trigger conditions in `docs/status/post-m001-content-backlog.md` (M002 Group B), and (b) a "same task, same scoring rule, often enough to beat noise" score requires the spine to **re-serve the same focal drill across weeks** — a scheduling guarantee the pure-spine does not make. **Keep the drill score as the pre-planned seam; if the founder wants it core, the plan must own a Phase 2B trigger and a same-drill repeat cadence.** (Refines the meta-synthesis's "courtside-capture cost" framing.)
- **F2 — "Planned-vs-completed" has no persisted "planned" denominator in Dexie v6.** Sessions are generated on demand; nothing persists an intended weekly count or forward queue, and the thin-spine ("only the next session is ever concrete") records no planned denominator by design. So the metric the meta-synthesis calls the unanimous best anchor **cannot be computed from already-captured data**, colliding with the milestone's "use only already-captured data" line (118). Resolve before treating it as in-scope: either define "planned" from a new persisted cadence-target/queue (a schema add the milestone currently forbids — and one that risks the calendar-guilt anti-pattern the metric note and ideation both name, per adversarial A4), or redefine the behavioral anchor to something derivable (e.g., sessions-completed in a rolling 2-4-week window, no ratio).
- **F3 — The staleness-ordered backlog rides existing data only for `pass`/`serve`/`set`** (3 of 7 `SkillFocus` values); `movement`/`conditioning` are never recorded as a session focus and `warmup`/`recovery` are focus-agnostic slots. Scope the v1 backlog to the three captured focuses explicitly; route fuller-taxonomy staleness to the deferred HM1 versioned-taxonomy primitive.
- **F4 — "Markdown-first emission" is v1-feasible only as a formatter over the existing typed model** (like `services/export/sessionExport.ts`), **not** as a persisted source from which typed objects are derived — the latter reopens the strict inward-pointing data-access layer contract (`.cursor/rules/data-access.mdc`). The plan must state which it means.
- **F5 — "Relabeling confidence to felt-readiness" is net-new capture, not a relabel.** No confidence/readiness field exists on `SessionReview` today. Surfacing it requires one optional review-side field + an off-RunScreen NRS at review — small and additive, but the plan must either carve a single-new-field exception into the milestone's "already-captured only" line or keep confidence out of the v1 receipt.
- **F6 — The only skill signal captured uniformly across all focuses is the `DifficultyTag` distribution** (`too_hard`/`still_learning`/`too_easy`); count-based proxies are blank on serve/set/streak drills. Define the v1 skill proxy off the difficulty-tag distribution, or label it as sparse/count-only.

## Verdict

**The thin-spine architecture is validated and right-sized.** The periodization physical-null reclaims the heavy-planner budget; the meta-synthesis and scope-guardian both read the pure-spine as the **evidence-backed ceiling, not a thin compromise**. Markdown-first emission (as a formatter, F4), the deferral of anchor/event/taper, and the no-AI-plan-gen / ownership posture are all directly validated. The premature-substrate flags (HM1 taxonomy refactor; typed cue-ladder *with* mastery gate) confirm the ideation's existing deferrals and argue against creep toward them.

**Three things need founder re-ratification before planning, because the evidence landed after the selection (adversarial A3):**

1. **The #5 deferral (S1)** — the founder's own #1 need ("see it adapting / 1% better") is under-served by #2 alone; the minimal offered-verdict + visible-carry-forward slice likely belongs in v1.
2. **The confidence framing (S2)** — reframe what confidence may claim (felt readiness, not skill proof); make planned-vs-completed the behavioral headline; reconcile the receipt to two user-facing metrics. Rename optional, meaning-correction mandatory.
3. **The all-content deferral (S3)** — pair the spine with warmup (#7) and seriously weigh the exploratory cue-ladder (#3); deferring *all* content runs against the primary new signal and the founder's own boldness mandate, and may not move the recommend-threshold the cohort decision rides on.

Net: this is **not** a "the approach is wrong" verdict. It is a "the architecture is right; the v1 scope was locked one step before the evidence, and three scope/framing calls should be re-ratified with that evidence in hand" verdict.

## The founder advancement-signal question, answered

The founder's #1 ask grades cleanly against the evidence (full table in the meta-synthesis §"founder advancement-signal question"):

- **Confidence alone** — weakest objective signal; honest as readiness, dishonest as skill proof; can move opposite to skill at the 2-5-yr band.
- **Reps / sessions done** — proves activity, not improvement.
- **Repeated structured drill score on a focal skill** — the most believable "1% better," but seam-only (F1: needs Phase 2B shape + same-drill repeat cadence).
- **Visible plan adaptation (carry-forward + offered verdict loop, survivor #5)** — the honest v1 "you can see it adapting" surface.

**Answer:** carry the founder's felt-progress in v1 with **(a) survivor #5's visible carry-forward + offered verdict loop**, **(b) a behavioral receipt** (planned-vs-completed *if* the denominator question F2 is resolved, else a rolling completed-sessions read), and **(c) confidence relabeled as a readiness companion** — with a **clean seam** for the repeated drill score as the objective upgrade. Confidence must not be asked to carry "I'm getting better."
